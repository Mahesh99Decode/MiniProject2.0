🌟 HIGHEST ENROLLMENT SCHEME

PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi Yojana

Direct income support of ₹6,000 per annum credited in three equal installments of ₹2,000 every 4 months directly into Aadhaar-linked bank accounts of all landholding farmer families.

✅ 100% DBT Transfer ₹6,000 / Year Next Installment: Nov 2026
☀️ Up to 75% Subsidy

PM-KUSUM Solar Agri-Pump Scheme

Subsidy for standalone solar agriculture pumps (3HP to 7.5HP) replacing diesel pumps for clean off-grid irrigation.

💰 Benefit: 60% Govt subsidy + 30% Bank loan
📜 Eligibility: Farmers with agricultural land
💧 55% - 80% Subsidy

Per Drop More Crop (Drip Irrigation)

Financial subsidy for installing micro-irrigation drip and sprinkler setups to enhance water-use efficiency by up to 50%.

💰 Benefit: Up to ₹45,000 / hectare subsidy
📜 Eligibility: All landholding cultivators
🛡️ ₹1 Premium / Acre

PM Fasal Bima Yojana (Crop Insurance)

Comprehensive insurance cover against crop loss due to drought, unseasonal floods, hailstorms, pest attacks, and post-harvest damages.

💰 Premium: Only 1.5% for Rabi, 2% for Kharif
📜 Settlement: Direct satellite claim assessment
🌾 50% Seed Subsidy

National Food Security Mission (NFSM)

Certified high-yield variety seeds distributed at 50% concession through local Krishi Seva Kendras for wheat, pulses, and oilseeds.

💰 Benefit: Free seed mini-kits for pulses
📜 Collection: Block Agriculture Office / PACS
